Nexus Legends Cookie Policy

Cookie Policy

Last updated: September 2026

What this covers

Cookies are small files a site stores on your device. Apps and sites also use "local storage", which works the same way. This page lists what Nexus Legends actually uses.

What we use: essential storage only

These are needed to run the service. They are not used for advertising or third-party tracking.

  • Sign-in session (in local storage)
    • Purpose: Keeps you signed in to your Player Profile or Captain Dressing Room and keeps your requests secure.
    • Lasts: Until you log out, or clear browser data.
  • Cookie notice choice
    • Purpose: Remembers your choice on the cookie consent notice so it doesn't ask on every visit.
    • Lasts: Until you clear it.
  • Screen preferences (e.g. dismissed celebration banners, read community announcements, cached team views, and active dashboard tabs)
    • Purpose: Keeps the app fast and remembers banners you've already seen.
    • Lasts: Until you clear it.

What we do not use

We do not set advertising cookies, marketing trackers, or A/B-testing cookies, and we do not use Meta, TikTok, or external ad networks.

Third-party content that may set its own cookies

  • Sign in with Google — Google's own sign-in page may set cookies while you authenticate your profile.
  • YouTube and Twitch — only when you open an embedded stream or replay inside our Media Hub. Their respective policies apply to those players.
  • ImgBB — used when uploading avatar photos, squad screenshots, or giveaway verification proofs.

Your choices

You can clear cookies and local storage data in your browser or phone settings at any time; doing so will sign you out and the cookie notice will appear again. Blocking essential storage stops sign-in and profile features from working.

Contact

If you have any questions about your data or privacy, contact us via the Admin Modmail Support or through our Welcome & Contact Portal.
